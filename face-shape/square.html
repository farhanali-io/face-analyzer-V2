Square Face Shape

By AI Face Analyzer · Updated September 8, 2026 · Reference choices

A square contour has forehead, cheek and jaw widths that stay close to each other, with a jaw that turns a defined corner rather than curving. The vertical sides and the strong horizontal jaw line are what make the outline read as square, and both are angle features rather than width features.

How to tell if your face is square

These visual cues can be checked in a mirror. If several do not fit, compare the neighbouring profiles below.

  • 01

    Forehead, cheekbone and jaw widths are all within a narrow range of one another.

  • 02

    There is a distinct corner where the jaw turns toward the chin, often visible below the ear.

  • 03

    The chin has a flat front edge rather than a point or a curve.

  • 04

    Face length is only modestly greater than width.

What tends to suit a square face

These are visual-balance conventions, not rules. They describe where volume, width or a line is usually placed to work with or against the natural contour — and either direction is a valid choice.

Hairstyles

  • •Soft layers and waves can contrast with a straighter jaw contour.
  • •A side-swept fringe adds a curved line near the forehead.
  • •Keep some movement around the temples and jaw.
Run the hairstyle finder →

Glasses

  • •Round, oval or softly curved frames contrast with angular contours.
  • •Thinner rims can keep the frame from feeling visually heavy.
  • •Choose enough width to avoid temple pressure.
Run the glasses finder →

Beard shapes

  • •A rounded or slightly tapered beard softens the corner if that is the goal.
  • •A sharply squared-off beard follows and reinforces the existing jaw line.
  • •Keeping the cheek line slightly higher and softer avoids adding width at the widest point.

Contour and makeup

  • •Contour along the jaw corner is the usual approach when softening is the goal.
  • •Rounded blush placement on the apple of the cheek contrasts with the angular outline.
  • •A softly curved brow rather than a strongly angled one balances the existing lines.

Earrings, necklines and hats

  • •Round and oval earrings contrast with the jaw line; hoops work particularly well.
  • •Scoop and rounded necklines echo curves rather than repeating the horizontal jaw.
  • •Round or rimless frames soften; heavy rectangular frames repeat the existing angles.

About the square shape

Measure your own face free →
How do I know if I have a square jaw?+

Trace from just below your ear down to your chin. If the line changes direction at a clear point rather than curving through, that corner is what defines a square contour. It is an angle, so it is visible in a mirror even when width measurements are inconclusive.

Is a square face shape only masculine?+

No. A defined jaw corner appears across all faces and carries no inherent gender. The styling conventions attached to it are cultural, and this page presents them as options rather than requirements.
