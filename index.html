          AI face rating, explained

          A face score describes the visible geometry in a photo. It is not a measure of personal worth, health or universal attractiveness.

          About AI Face Analyzer →
          Is the AI face rating free?+

          Yes, and it is unlimited. The complete local report on this page is free, has no usage counter, no weekly subscription and no account. Test as many photos as you want.

          Is my photo uploaded or stored?+

          No. The free analyzer runs the face-landmark model, pixel checks and scoring formulas in this browser. Your photo, landmarks and local result are not sent to our server.

          Why can my score change between photos?+

          Because a combined score mixes two things: your facial geometry and the quality of the photograph. Lighting, lens distance, head angle, expression and sharpness all move a 2D measurement. Every result here splits face geometry from photo presentation, and the Face Score Consistency Test measures that split across two or three photos so you can see which one actually changed.

          How is the score calculated?+

          ARF Local Geometry v3.0 combines visible symmetry (30%), proportion balance (25%), feature balance (25%) and photo presentation (20%) from local landmarks and image-quality signals.

          What photo works best?+

          Use one adult face, straight-on, in soft even light. Keep the full face visible and avoid sunglasses, strong filters, wide-angle close-ups and dramatic shadows.

          Can minors use the tool?+

          No. This service is only for adults aged 18 or older, and photos of minors must not be uploaded.
