Evidence behind this result

The output is built from inspectable photo geometry and a separate image-quality signal.

01

Contour angles

Estimates the visible turn from cheek-side contour through the jaw toward the chin.

02

Side difference

Reports the absolute difference between left and right photo angles.

03

Lower-face proportions

Adds jaw-to-cheek width and the lower third of visible face length.

How this tool works

  1. 1. Trace

    Five contour landmarks form the visible cheek–jaw–chin line.

  2. 2. Calculate

    Angles and scale-independent width ratios are computed locally.

  3. 3. Summarise

    Balance and side agreement contribute to an explainable contour index.

What the result cannot tell you

01

This is not a measure of skeletal anatomy, health, masculinity, femininity or attractiveness.

02

Facial hair, neck posture, body composition, shadows and lens distortion affect the contour.

03

A side-to-side photo difference can come from pose rather than the face itself.

About this tool

How local processing works →
What does the jawline score represent?+

It is a photo-based contour-balance index based on side agreement and visible jaw-to-cheek proportions.

Does facial hair affect it?+

Yes. Beards and strong shadows can move or hide the contour landmarks and reduce reliability.

Is this a medical measurement?+

No. It is an entertainment and self-exploration tool for visible photo geometry only.
