    01

    A photo cannot measure bridge fit, pupillary distance, temple width or prescription requirements.

    02

    Frame colour, material, scale and personal expression can matter as much as shape.

    03

    Always use measurements and advice from a qualified optician for prescription eyewear.

    How local processing works →
    Can this tell me the exact frame size?+

    No. A single photo has no reliable physical scale. Use an optician's measurements for size and optical alignment.

    Do I have to choose the suggested shape?+

    No. Contrast-based frame advice is a styling convention, not a rule.

    What matters most for prescription glasses?+

    Optical alignment, bridge and temple fit, lens requirements and comfort come before the face-shape suggestion.
